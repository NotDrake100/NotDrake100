### Selected work

**Audits**

- [**kernelarena-strict-check**](https://github.com/NotDrake100/kernelarena-strict-check) A stricter correctness check for AI-written GPU kernels on KernelArena. 6 of the 16 entries I tested passed every normal check, then failed once the weights changed between calls.
- [**instinctrazor-capability-damage**](https://github.com/NotDrake100/instinctrazor-capability-damage) Scored a sub-4-bit MoE quantizer one capability at a time instead of one average. Factual recall broke far more than arithmetic. Keeping layer 0 at higher precision costs 0.064 bits per weight and wins back about 21 points of recall on held-out items.

**Research**

**Builds**
